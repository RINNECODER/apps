BPM Counter — Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

The short version

BPM Counter does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing leaves your iPhone.

Microphone

With your permission, the app uses the microphone to detect the tempo of the music around you. The audio is analyzed on your device in real time, is not recorded or saved, and is never sent anywhere. Tap mode works without the microphone.

What stays on your device

Your settings, history and purchase status are saved only on your iPhone.

Purchases

Purchases ($2.99 per week, $4.99 per month or $12.99 once) are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details. Manage or cancel any time in iPhone Settings, under your Apple ID, then Subscriptions.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
