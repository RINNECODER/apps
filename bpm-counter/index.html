Common questions

How does it find the tempo? It listens through the microphone and analyzes the audio on your iPhone. Tap mode works without the microphone.

What is free? 30 seconds of listening per day. Pro unlocks unlimited listening ($2.99 per week, $4.99 per month or $12.99 once).

Restore a purchase

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple ID you purchased with. To cancel a subscription, open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
