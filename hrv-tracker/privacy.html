HRV Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

The short version

HRV Tracker does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing leaves your iPhone.

Apple Health

With your permission, the app reads heart rate variability, resting heart rate and sleep from Apple Health. Access is read-only: the app never writes to Apple Health. This data is processed on your device to draw your charts and insights, and is never sent anywhere.

What stays on your device

Your settings and purchase status are saved only on your iPhone.

Purchases

Subscriptions ($19.99 per year or $2.99 per month) are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details; the app only receives confirmation that a subscription is active. Manage or cancel any time in iPhone Settings, under your Apple ID, then Subscriptions.

Not a medical device

HRV Tracker is for general wellness information only. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Talk to a health professional about medical concerns.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: open a request on GitHub.
