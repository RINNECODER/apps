Common questions

Where does the data come from? Apple Health. The app reads heart rate variability, resting heart rate and sleep, read-only. HRV comes from an Apple Watch or another device that records it. If a chart is empty, check Settings, Health, Data Access & Devices, HRV Tracker.

What is free? Today and 7-day Trends. Premium ($19.99 per year or $2.99 per month) adds longer Trends and Insights.

Medical disclaimer: HRV Tracker is for general wellness only and is not a medical device.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple ID you subscribed with. To cancel, open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
