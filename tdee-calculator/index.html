Common questions

How is it calculated? On your iPhone, from the age, sex, height, weight and activity level you enter. Optionally you can import height, weight, age and sex from Apple Health, read-only.

What is free? Your maintenance calories. Premium ($6.99 per month, $24.99 per year or $49.99 once) unlocks the goal calorie target and macros.

Medical disclaimer: TDEE Calculator gives general estimates only and is not medical advice or a medical device.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple ID you purchased with. To cancel a subscription, open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
