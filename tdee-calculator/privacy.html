TDEE Calculator — Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

The short version

TDEE Calculator does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing leaves your iPhone.

What you enter

Your age, sex, height, weight and activity level are used to calculate your calories on your device. They are saved only on your iPhone.

Apple Health

If you choose to import, the app reads height, weight, date of birth and biological sex from Apple Health. Access is read-only: the app never writes to Apple Health. This data is used on your device to fill in the calculator and is never sent anywhere.

Purchases

Premium purchases ($6.99 per month, $24.99 per year or $49.99 once) are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details; the app only receives confirmation that Premium is active. Manage or cancel any time in iPhone Settings, under your Apple ID, then Subscriptions.

Not medical advice

TDEE Calculator provides general estimates only. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Talk to a health professional about medical or dietary concerns.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
