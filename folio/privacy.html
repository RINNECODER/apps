Folio — Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

The short version

Folio does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Every document you convert, scan, sign or merge is processed on your iPhone and saved in the app's own storage. Nothing is uploaded to us. Camera and photo access are used only when you choose to scan or import, and only on the device. When you convert a web page, the app loads that page directly from its website, like a browser. Face ID, if you turn it on, is handled by iOS; the app never sees your face data.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details; the app only receives confirmation that a purchase is active.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: open a request on GitHub.
