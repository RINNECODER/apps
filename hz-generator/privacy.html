Hz Generator — Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

The short version

Hz Generator does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your settings, saved presets, free-time counter and purchase status are saved only on your iPhone. The app generates tones; it does not use the microphone.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details; the app only receives confirmation that a purchase is active.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions: open a request on GitHub.
